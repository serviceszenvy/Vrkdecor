About VRK Decor

Making Nagercoil celebrate, since 2011

VRK Decor is a Nagercoil event decoration house, trusted by families across Kanyakumari district for weddings, functions and corporate events

Who we are

Design, build and run · all under one roof

Founded in December 2011, we have designed and delivered more than 700 celebrations with an in-house team of 35, from intimate housewarmings to full wedding weekends

Design, stage and mandap work, florals, entrances, furniture and seating are handled by our own crew. Sound, lighting, band set and other specialities come through long-standing partner vendors, with VRK as your single point of accountability throughout
